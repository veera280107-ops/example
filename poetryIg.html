Till Spring Came Alive

Poem by Veera

she searched for places,
arable lands,
holding it hopefully,
in her bare hands.

lush green plains,
wind-swept meadows,
but for what she believed to be doom,
nothing could make the bud bloom.

she walked the silence,
with chaos in head,
till her senses would succumb
to all that she bared.

weary and famished, on a hay sack she lay,
right after the storms had faded away.

and there in the quiet, beneath silver skies,
she loosened the sorrow she carried inside.
for roots do not deepen in terror or haste,
nor flowers awaken when smothered by pain.

the earth did not ask her to fight to survive,
it simply stayed patient till spring came alive.
and slowly, unnoticed, through cracks in the gloom,
the smallest of petals had started to bloom.
